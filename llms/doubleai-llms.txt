# doubleAI

> doubleAI (legal entity AAI Technologies Ltd.) is an Israeli AI research company building Artificial Expert Intelligence (AEI) — AI systems that concentrate full training capacity on a single technical domain to reach and surpass leading human experts. Its flagship system, WarpSpeed, is an AI GPU performance engineer that beats NVIDIA's optimized PyTorch baselines on the majority of tested kernels. Founded in late 2023 by Prof. Amnon Shashua and Prof. Shai Shalev-Shwartz. doubleAI publishes research and open-source code but does not currently expose a public developer API.
